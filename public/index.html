How to play

You get two words. Type one word that sits right between them in meaning.

  • Balance: how evenly your word splits between the two. 50:50 is perfect.
  • Relevance: how close your word is to both. An unrelated word can be balanced and still score nothing.
  • Score = balance × relevance, out of 100.

Five pairs a day, one guess each, and they get stranger as you go. Everyone gets the same pairs.

Word meanings come from ConceptNet Numberbatch (CC BY-SA 4.0). Rarity is based on how often a word appears in film and TV subtitles.
